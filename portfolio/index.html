Selected work

CAR T-cell therapy in glioblastoma

Lead author · independent · Sep 2025 to present

I'm leading a systematic review and meta-analysis of early-phase CAR T-cell trials in glioblastoma, with GRADE assessment. The protocol is registered on PROSPERO (CRD420251237673). My first-author abstract was accepted at the SITC 41st Annual Meeting and at YURC 2027, and the manuscript is being prepared for resubmission.

Is lineage confounding inflating cancer dependency biomarkers?

Independent · computational biology · 2026

I reanalyzed public DepMap data to work out how much of the apparent robustness of cancer dependency biomarkers comes from lineage confounding and how much from statistical power. The work was accepted as a poster at the 2026 IEEE MIT URTC, and a follow-up on benchmarking robustness metrics was accepted at YURC 2027. I've also measured reproducibility ceilings in ChEMBL binding-affinity data, tested for Purkinje cell loss in essential tremor with GEO cerebellum data, and compared adverse-event reporting by sex across 52 drugs in FAERS.

Prediabetes lesson for 500 high school students

Columbia University ACComPLISHED Program · Summer 2025

I designed a prediabetes lesson, delivered it to more than 500 students, and tested them before and after. Average scores rose from 5.88 to 8.92 (p < 0.001, r = 0.84). I presented the results at Columbia University Irving Medical Center.

Meta-analysis for patients with limited English

ThinkNeuro SML Project · Nov 2025 to present

I screened over 1,600 articles, ran the meta-analysis in R, sorted 80+ studies for the meta-ethnography, and drafted the manuscript, which is now a preprint on Research Square. I was ranked first of the program's 172 interns.
